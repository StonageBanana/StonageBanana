#### [Markerless vs. Marker-Based Motion Capture — SAM 3D Body vs. Vicon](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Movement-Analyses-sam3d-vs-mocap) · *2026*
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Computer%20Vision-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/3D%20Geometry-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Biomechanics-58A6FF?style=flat-square" />

Tested whether a two-camera markerless setup can substitute for a 10-camera Vicon rig in clinical gait analysis. Root-caused a systematic geometric distortion present in every trial — hip keypoints compressed to ~44% of true width, pelvis axis ~106° misaligned — cutting hip and knee flexion RMSE by **66–75%** (knee: 84° → 21°) and flipping correlation with ground truth from strongly negative to strongly positive. Nine-stage reproducible pipeline with a regression-audit suite enforcing mathematical invariants; two-view fusion requiring **zero camera calibration**.

#### [Custom CNN Architectures for Histopathological Cancer Classification](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Customized-CNNs-for-Precise-Lung-Cancer-Classification) · *2024*
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Medical%20Imaging-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PyTorch%20%2F%20Keras-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Grad--CAM-58A6FF?style=flat-square" />

Three architectures benchmarked on the 25,000-image LC25000 dataset — from-scratch baseline, Custom ResNet with attention + ImageNet transfer, Custom EfficientNet with compound scaling. **97.2% accuracy** with EfficientNet, plus Grad-CAM heatmaps so predictions are reviewable by a pathologist — the prerequisite for any clinical deployment.

#### [Pathfinding: Classical Search vs. RL vs. Sampling Planners](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Pathfinding-Algorithms) · *2025*
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Reinforcement%20Learning-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Graph%20Algorithms-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/OOP%20Design-58A6FF?style=flat-square" />

Twelve planners across three algorithmic families behind a single abstract base class — A\*, Dijkstra, JPS, Q-Learning, SARSA, DQN, RRT, RRT\*. **DQN built from scratch in PyTorch** (replay buffer, target network, ε-greedy decay, Bellman loss). Real-time Pygame visualization makes on-policy vs. off-policy divergence directly observable.

#### [Flight Price Prediction — Deployed ML Web Application](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Flight-Price-Prediction-SmartBridge-Internship---Applied-Data-Science) · *2023*
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Feature%20Engineering-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/CatBoost-58A6FF?style=flat-square" /> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Deployed-4CAF50?style=flat-square" />

Eight regression algorithms compared on 10,000+ records; **CatBoost at ~96% R²**, chosen because ordered target statistics handle high-cardinality categoricals natively — avoiding both the dimensionality explosion of one-hot encoding and the false ordinality of label encoding. **[Try it live →](https://stonagebanana-flight-price-prediction-smartb.hf.space/)**

**[Crop Yield Prediction from Climate and Agronomic Data](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Crop-Yield-Prediction)** · *2024*
Merged four independently-sourced agricultural datasets into a single modelling table, reconciling inconsistent country naming, misaligned year coverage, differing units, and join-induced missingness. Five regressors benchmarked; Random Forest selected, with the gap to the linear baseline quantifying the non-linearity of the system.

**[Behavioural Segmentation of Credit Card Customers](https://github.com/StonageBanana/Novel-Customer-Segmentation-on-Credit-Card-Customer-Details)** · *2023*
~9,000 cardholders segmented on 18 behavioural variables. Four clustering algorithms with deliberately incompatible assumptions compared — agreement between assumption-free Mean Shift and fixed-*k* K-Means used as evidence that the discovered structure was real rather than imposed. Translated into targeted marketing, credit-risk flagging, and retention actions.
